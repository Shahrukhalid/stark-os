An AI social media team that asks before it posts

STARK OS is a free AI social media manager you run on your own computer. Seven AI agents plan campaigns, write platform-native posts, triage mentions, research trends, stress-test content for backlash and schedule it, on free AI models. Nothing goes out until you approve the exact text.

Why people use STARK OS

Free, not a subscription

A self-hosted alternative to paid social media schedulers and AI writing tools. It runs on free OpenRouter models with automatic failover.

Autopilot

Set a campaign once. It finds fresh stories, picks SEO keywords and hashtags, writes and fact-checks a post per platform and schedules it. Approve the batch in one click, or let posts go out unless you cancel.

You stay in control

Every post is approved by you, or by a trust level you chose. Sensitive news always waits for you, any edit cancels an approval, and campaigns pause themselves if something looks wrong.

A team, not one chatbot

Seven agents with separate jobs check each other's work, including a red-team agent that asks "how could this backfire?".

Rules in code

Every draft is checked for length limits, leaked passwords and keys, personal data, missing #ad disclosure and giveaway rules.

Real posting

Publishes threads and replies to Bluesky and Mastodon. For X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and YouTube it writes ready-to-paste posts.

Watch the AI work

A live view shows every agent thinking, calling tools and handing work to a teammate on an animated agent network, as it happens.

Calendar and analytics

Drag posts onto a content calendar. See real likes, reposts and replies, your best time to post, your top posts, and export it all as CSV.

Listen and repurpose

Follow keywords, hashtags and competitors, get replies drafted for you, and turn one blog post or video into posts for every platform, with free AI images.

Approve from your phone

Telegram messages with Approve and Publish buttons, a daily digest, and A/B hook tests that teach the team what works.

Private by design

Runs on your machine with a local database. The API only listens on 127.0.0.1 and needs a random token.

Install in two commands

Needs Python 3.11+, Node.js 20+ and a free OpenRouter key.

git clone https://github.com/Shahrukhalid/stark-os.git && cd stark-os
make setup   # then add OPENROUTER_API_KEY to .env
make dev     # open the dashboard it prints

Questions

Is STARK OS free?

Yes. It is MIT licensed and runs on free OpenRouter models. It never calls a paid model unless you turn that on.

Which social networks does it support?

It posts for real to Bluesky and Mastodon through their free APIs, and writes ready-to-paste posts for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok and YouTube.

Can the AI post without me?

No. Every post needs your approval of the exact text. Scheduled posts go out automatically only if you approved that text for that time.

Where are my logins stored?

Only on your computer, in a file readable by your user account. There is no cloud service.
