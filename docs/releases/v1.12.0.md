# STARK AI 1.12.0

**STARK OS is now STARK AI.** Same app, same team, new name. Your settings, connected accounts, posts and history carry over.

**Updating from 1.11 or older:** download the installer below once and install it over the old app. (Older versions can't follow the new project address, so they won't offer this update themselves.) From 1.12.0 on, updates arrive inside the app again. On a Mac you can delete the old "STARK OS" app from Applications afterwards; on Windows the installer removes it for you.

## New
- **Vision and Wanda**, two assistants you talk to by name. Vision helps with strategy, analytics and account health; Wanda writes posts, captions and campaigns. Both also help with general questions, can draft and check posts, read analytics, and call in the seven specialists after you approve what they share. Publishing still needs your approval.
- **Logins that don't run out.** Instagram and Threads logins are renewed about once a week, Pinterest through STARK Connect. LinkedIn (which can't be renewed) warns you 10 days before it ends.
- **Real engagement** for Instagram (likes, comments), YouTube (likes, comments, views) and Pinterest (reactions, saves, comments, impressions, clicks).
- **Encrypted logins on your computer.** The desktop app encrypts saved accounts and keys; the key lives in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager.

## Fixes
- Security: sign-in pages escape their links and show only cleaned error messages; Diagnostics no longer shows full crash logs; the PDF reader is updated to a patched version.
- A provider you pinned is always respected; connection errors never include tokens.
- Analytics loads faster; social listening errors are logged.

Installers are not signed yet: on a Mac right-click → **Open** the first time; on Windows choose **More info → Run anyway**.
