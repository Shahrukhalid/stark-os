# Code of conduct

STARK OS is a friendly, welcoming project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project) agrees to these standards.

## Our standards

Do:
- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

Don't:
- Harass, insult or demean anyone, including through sexualised language, slurs or personal attacks.
- Share anyone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, logins) without their explicit permission.
- Troll, spam, or derail discussions.
- Use this project to build tools for impersonation, fake engagement, harassment or surveillance of people.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@Shahrukhalid](https://github.com/Shahrukhalid), privately on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

## Enforcement

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may give a private warning, ask for a public correction, remove comments or contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project.
